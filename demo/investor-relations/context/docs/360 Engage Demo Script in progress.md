360 Engage Demo Script

Overall Vision: We are creating the world's premier fan engagement platform. 

Initially: We are focused on live experiences. We want to increase the money sports stadiums make per seat. Target: Double or more. 

Later: We want to be the all in one sports fan assistance app - whether it's during the game or in between games, fans can ask for information, get purchase recommendations, and be constantly engaged and feel celebrated.

QR Code Based vs Full App
QR Code Based is perfect for live experience engagement. 
But, QR Code Based / Web App cannot engage fans outside of games. 
We could have a workaround, like sending people texts with a link to open the app and engage, but it may have challenges 
App is nice, but has downsides: needs to be downloaded, needs compliance with apple and google, maintenance, etc. 
Other workarounds 
Agentic interactions over sms, over whatsapp, over email, etc (will take time and budget) 

AI Integration:
Text AI
Doable, but requires heavy guardrails.
Can be jailbroken and attacked
Any functionality including agenticness carries high risks 
Voice AI
Challenges 
Will be expensive (about 10 cents a minute per fan) 
Won't work during games (too much noise from other fans and game)
Initially, the QR code based web app will only be useful during live games. 
Will need robust security, because voice is biometric data, and can be used for all sorts of nefarious purposes, including identity theft, where their voice can be replicated with AI and people can be called sounding like them. 
Will not be useful until we first have the app being functional, with payment, security, etc integrated. Then, we integrate text, and then, we look at voice.
