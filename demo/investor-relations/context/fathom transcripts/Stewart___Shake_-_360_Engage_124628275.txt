  - [**Pivot from Voice to Chat:** The MVP will use a chat interface, not voice. This avoids high operational costs (\~$1/fan), phone permission issues, and unreliable stadium data.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=781.0)
  - [**Phased Rollout:** The product will launch in phases (e.g., Food/Drink → Merch → Tickets) to manage complexity and build a defensible position by integrating with club POS systems.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1707.0)
  - [**Illustrative Demo:** The current demo will be updated to show the *potential* of a full product, using placeholder buttons for features like "Incident Report" to secure Letters of Intent (LOIs) for the MVP.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=2042.0)
  - [**Revenue Model:** A revenue-share model (e.g., 5–10% of sales) aligns incentives, making the product a direct path for clubs to monetize their fan base.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1781.0)

## Topics

### Voice vs. Chat Interface

  - [**Initial Request:** The team requested a voice interface for a "companion" experience.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=522.0)
  - [**Decision:** The MVP will use a chat interface.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1642.0)
  - [**Rationale:** Voice is too complex and costly for an MVP.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=781.0)
      - [**Operational Cost:** \~$1/fan for 10 mins vs. negligible cost for chat.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=781.0)
      - [**Technical Issues:** Phone permissions, battery drain, and unreliable stadium data.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1069.0)
      - [**Reliability:** Chat via web app works for 100% of users, ensuring a stable experience.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1058.0)

### MVP Scope & Phased Rollout

  - [**Strategy:** Launch with a focused MVP and expand features over time.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1707.0)
  - [**Phase 1 MVP:** Food & Drink ordering.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1448.0)
      - [**Rationale:** A simple, high-revenue feature that provides immediate value.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1448.0)
      - [**Process:** Requires integrating with diverse stadium POS systems, which is a key challenge but also a long-term competitive advantage.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1736.0)
  - [**Future Phases:** Merch, Tickets, Auctions, Donations, and personalized offers.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1986.0)
      - [**Timeline:** A full-featured app could take 2+ years.](https://fathom.video/share/n1Lpz9nDtaPCDmCaqdrzxeXc1d38cMyr?tab=summary&timestamp=1939.0)
