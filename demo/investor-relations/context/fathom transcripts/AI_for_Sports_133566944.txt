## Topics

### Voice AI Demo: The New Priority

  - [The voice AI demo is now the top priority for sales, driven by strong prospect interest (e.g., Graham spent 18 hours of golf discussing voice activation on 12 holes with a contact).](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=43.0)
  - [Shekhar confirmed a live, interactive demo is easier to build than a pre-recorded AI video, stating he would "rather give you guys a voice station."](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=342.0)
  - [**Demo Scenario:** A fan from the US planning a trip to a match, as this is a common and effective conversation starter for demonstrating capabilities.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=715.0)
      - [**Fan Request Example:** "I'm flying home for the match in two weeks; I need help with tickets, a hotel, and merchandise."](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=306.0)
      - [**Outcome:** The demo must show the voice interaction (transcript) and the resulting data captured by the system, demonstrating how the club picks up the phone at the other end.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=306.0)

### Demo Structure & Game-Day Chat

  - [The new demo flow, anticipated to be around 15 minutes, is designed to build excitement and leave prospects wanting more:](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=144.0)
    1.  [**Voice Activation:** Start with the most innovative feature, showcasing the direct interaction.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=144.0)
    2.  [**Game-Day Chat:** Follow with the nearly-complete, highly compelling game-day experience.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=199.0)
  - [The game-day chat is a key differentiator, leveraging real-time data to drive revenue and fan engagement.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=199.0)
      - [**Example:** If food sales are low pre-game or during the match, the QR code game day simulator should send a notification (e.g., "price drop in XYZ food") to encourage fans to stay after the game for discounted items (e.g., "burgers will be half price").](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=252.0)
      - [**Rationale:** This strategy helps clubs capture revenue from unsold inventory and trains fans to stay in the stadium longer, aligning with club goals (e.g., Arsenal's game-day revenue increased from £300k to £1M after building new stadium infrastructure for entertainment and food sales).](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=216.0)

### Pricing Model

  - [Shekhar initially proposed a transaction-based model ("eat-what-you-kill") over a subscription, believing it could generate more money for both the company and the clubs.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=586.0)
  - [The team decided to offer clubs a choice between the two models.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=632.0)
      - [**Rationale:** This flexible approach maximizes client appeal and revenue potential, allowing clubs to choose the model that best suits their financial structure.](https://fathom.video/share/qmT7ypGbzh_rHbTJXa_1hiEEXaHLS3nh?tab=summary&timestamp=632.0)
