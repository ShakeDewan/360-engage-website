A. Background and Purpose
A.1 This LOI records the Parties' intention to evaluate and, subject to successful testing and
mutual agreement, potentially pilot a digital fan engagement and insight platform currently
referred to as the 360Engage platform (Platform).
A.2 The Platform is developed and operated by Glasshouse with a technology partner,
BioSquares (as applicable). For clarity, the Club's relationship under this LOI is with
Glasshouse, unless and until the Parties agree otherwise in a definitive written agreement.
A.3 This LOI is intended to provide a structured framework for discussions and evaluation.
Except for the Binding Clauses (as defined in clause 10), this LOI is non-binding and does
not create legal obligations to proceed.
1. Scope of Evaluation and Intended Use Cases
1.1 The Club confirms its interest in evaluating the Platform for the following illustrative use
cases:
•supporter engagement (matchday and remote)
•digital activations and fan journeys
•sponsor value enhancement and activation reporting
•conversational commerce and merchandising interactions
•structured fan data capture, segmentation, and insight generation

1.2 Any progression from evaluation to a pilot programme is conditional on:
•successful completion of MVP or beta testing (as applicable)
•confirmation of operational suitability within the Club environment
•mutual alignment on objectives, scope, and implementation approach
•agreement of a definitive written pilot services agreement
2. Platform Status, Availability, and No Warranty
2.1 The Parties acknowledge that the Platform is currently in MVP and/or beta phase and is
being evaluated for validation and refinement.
2.2 The Parties acknowledge and agree that during evaluation and any pilot:
•modules, integrations, and automation features may have limited functionality
•availability and uptime may be limited, non-continuous, or subject to development
cycles
•features may change, be removed, or be iterated during testing
2.3 The Club acknowledges that, at this stage, Glasshouse does not provide any minimum
performance commitments, service levels, uptime guarantees, or warranties, unless and
until agreed in a definitive written agreement.
3. Proposed Pilot Framework (Illustrative Only)
3.1 Subject to successful evaluation, the Parties anticipate negotiating a pilot that may
include:
•supporter engagement modules for matchday and remote fans
•sponsor activation journeys and campaign tools
•conversational ticketing and merchandise interactions
•analytics dashboards and reporting
•integration across selected Club digital channels
3.2 The above is indicative only. It does not constitute a commitment to deliver any particular
modules, integrations, outcomes, or timelines.
4. Commercial Model
4.1 The commercial model for the Platform shall be agreed between the Parties and set out
in a definitive written pilot services agreement. This may include, but is not limited to,
subscription fees, revenue-share arrangements, data participation structures, sponsor
activation economics, or hybrid models.
4.2 No commercial terms are binding unless set out in a definitive written agreement signed
by both Parties.
4.3 Any indicative pricing, commercial structures, or revenue participation discussed during
evaluation are non-binding and subject to change.

5. Commercial Discussions (Non-Binding)
5.1 If evaluation meets the Parties' expectations, the Parties intend to negotiate in good faith
toward a pilot services agreement addressing, at minimum:
•pilot duration and milestones
•deliverables and scope
•integration responsibilities and access requirements
•support model and communications
•commercial model (subscription, revenue share, data participation, or hybrid)
•data protection and security obligations
•acceptable use, content moderation, and brand safeguards
•reporting, evidence standards, and claims controls (including ESG and impact claims,
if applicable)
5.2 No commercial terms are binding unless set out in a definitive written agreement signed
by both Parties.
6. Marketing Channel and Referral Concept (Non-Binding)
6.1 Subject to a definitive written agreement, the Parties may explore the Club acting as an
early adopter and reference club for the Platform.
6.2 The Parties may discuss a referral or introduction arrangement under which, where Club
introductions lead to executed commercial agreements, the Club may participate in agreed
economics relating to those referred clubs.
6.3 Any referral mechanics, revenue participation, and term must be set out in a separate
written agreement. The Club has no obligation to promote or introduce the Platform under
this LOI.
7. Publicity and Use of the LOI for Investor and Partner Discussions
7.1 Glasshouse may reference the existence of this LOI solely for investor discussions,
fundraising, strategic partner engagement, and technology development partnerships,
provided that the Club gives prior written consent (email sufficient) to the specific wording
used.
7.2 For clarity, unless the Club provides prior written consent:
•Glasshouse must not use the Club's name, crest, trademarks, or other brand assets
•Glasshouse must not state or imply that the Platform is endorsed by the Club
•Glasshouse must not disclose any confidential information, pilot data, or evaluation
feedback
7.3 If the Club provides consent to name use, the Parties will agree a short, factual form of
wording suitable for investor and partner contexts.
8. Data Protection, Fan Data, and Security (Principles Only)
8.1 The Parties acknowledge that evaluation and pilot activity may involve personal data
relating to supporters and other individuals. Any processing of personal data must comply

with applicable data protection legislation, including (as applicable) UK GDPR, the Data
Protection Act 2018, and any equivalent legislation in the Club's jurisdiction.
8.2 The Parties anticipate that:
•the Club will be the data controller for Club supporter data
•Glasshouse will act as a data processor to the extent it processes personal data on the
Club's behalf
•the Parties will enter into an appropriate data processing agreement before any live
processing of personal data begins
8.3 The Parties will discuss and document, in a definitive agreement, the minimum security
controls, access controls, hosting arrangements, retention periods, incident response, and
audit rights appropriate for the Club environment.
9. Intellectual Property and Feedback (Principles Only)
9.1 All intellectual property rights in and to the Platform, including updates and derivatives,
remain with Glasshouse and its licensors.
9.2 The Club retains all rights in its own intellectual property, data, and materials.
9.3 The Parties anticipate that feedback, suggestions, and evaluation learnings provided by
the Club may be used by Glasshouse to improve the Platform, provided that Glasshouse
does not disclose the Club's confidential information and does not attribute feedback to the
Club without consent.
10. Non-Binding Nature, Binding Clauses, and Exit Rights
10.1 Non-binding. Except for the Binding Clauses listed in clause 10.2, this LOI does not
create any legally binding obligations on either Party to proceed with evaluation, pilot, or any
commercial arrangement.
10.2 Binding Clauses. The following clauses are intended to be legally binding:
•clause 7 (Publicity and use of the LOI)
•clause 11 (Confidentiality)
•clause 12 (Governing law and jurisdiction)
•clause 13 (Costs)
•clause 14 (No partnership, no agency, no exclusivity)
10.3 Exit rights. Either Party may withdraw from discussions at any time prior to execution of
a definitive written agreement, without liability, subject to the Binding Clauses.
11. Confidentiality
11.1 Each Party may receive confidential information from the other in connection with this
LOI, evaluation, MVP access, or related discussions.
11.2 Each Party undertakes to keep the other Party's confidential information confidential
and not disclose it to any third party except:
•to its professional advisers who are bound by confidentiality obligations
•as required by law or a competent regulatory authority

## Page 5

•with the other Party's prior written consent
11.3 This clause remains in force for a period of two (2) years from the date of this LOI,
unless replaced or extended by a definitive agreement.
12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
12.1 This LOI and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it shall be
governed by the laws of England and Wales.
12.2 The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
13. Costs
13.1 Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with the negotiation,
evaluation, and preparation of any definitive agreements, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
14. No Partnership, No Agency, No Exclusivity
14.1 Nothing in this LOI creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, fiduciary relationship, or
employment relationship between the Parties.
14.2 Neither Party has authority to bind the other.
14.3 This LOI does not grant exclusivity. Either Party may discuss similar arrangements with
third parties.
15. Indicative Timelines (Non-Binding)
15.1 The Parties expect to coordinate MVP access, testing, feedback, and refinement within
a mutually agreed timeframe.
15.2 Any dates discussed are indicative only unless set out in a definitive written agreement.
