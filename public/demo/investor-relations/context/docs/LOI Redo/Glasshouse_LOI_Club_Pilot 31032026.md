1. Background

Glasshouse Group UK Ltd has developed 360Engage, a digital fan engagement, commerce, and insight platform for professional sports organisations. It is designed to support supporter interaction, campaign activation, first-party data capture, fan insight generation, and related commercial use cases.

The platform is developed and operated by Glasshouse with a technology partner, BioSquares, as applicable. For clarity, the Club's relationship under this Letter of Intent is with Glasshouse unless and until the Parties agree otherwise in a definitive written agreement.

The Parties wish to begin an initial discovery and pilot scoping process and, if they remain aligned, negotiate in good faith a statement of work and related pilot agreement for 360Engage within the Club's environment.

2. Purpose

This Letter of Intent records the basis on which the Parties intend to:

• run a structured discovery process to assess the Club's objectives, operational needs, and use cases;
• take part in pilot scoping and solution design discussions;
• prepare, in good faith, a draft statement of work covering scope, delivery approach, outputs, timeline, and commercial terms; and
• provide a framework for information sharing, confidentiality, and permitted publicity.

3. Nature of this Letter of Intent

Except for Clauses 9 to 11, this Letter of Intent is non-binding and records only the Parties' present intention. It does not require either Party to enter into any further agreement, proceed with any pilot, or complete any transaction or commercial arrangement.

Nothing in this Letter of Intent creates a partnership, joint venture, agency relationship, fiduciary relationship, or exclusivity between the Parties.

Either Party may withdraw from discussions at any time before a definitive written pilot agreement is signed, without liability except for the binding obligations in this Letter of Intent.

4. Discovery and Pilot Scoping Process

After this Letter of Intent is signed, the Parties shall use reasonable endeavours to work constructively through discovery and pilot scoping.

That process is expected to include:

• an initial discovery session between Glasshouse and the Club's nominated representatives;
• discussion of the Club's supporter engagement, sponsorship, merchandising, data, and operational goals;
• identification of pilot use cases, user journeys, technical touchpoints, and implementation assumptions;
• review of data protection, security, governance, and approval requirements; and
• preparation and refinement of a draft statement of work.

Each Party shall nominate appropriate personnel to take part in that process and shall negotiate the proposed statement of work in good faith. Good faith negotiation does not require either Party to accept terms it does not consider acceptable.

5. Proposed Pilot Framework

Subject to agreement of a definitive written pilot agreement and statement of work, the proposed pilot may include selected elements of the 360Engage platform relevant to the Club's priorities. These may include supporter engagement journeys, sponsor activation workflows, conversational commerce, campaign reporting, and insight dashboards.

Any description of the proposed pilot in this Letter of Intent is indicative only. No functionality, integration, timeline, performance commitment, service level, fee, or commercial outcome is agreed or guaranteed unless it is set out in a definitive written agreement signed by both Parties.

6. Platform Status and No Reliance

The Club acknowledges that, as at the date of this Letter of Intent, the platform may include MVP, beta, or developmental components. Features, modules, integrations, and workflows may be updated, refined, withdrawn, or replaced during discovery and pilot scoping.

Accordingly, Glasshouse gives no representation, warranty, or undertaking in this Letter of Intent as to platform availability, uptime, suitability for a particular purpose, or achievement of any commercial, operational, or technical result.

7. Data Protection, Data Ownership and Analytics

The Parties acknowledge that any personal data relating to supporters or other individuals processed in connection with any pilot shall be handled in accordance with applicable data protection law, including UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and any additional laws applicable to the Club's jurisdiction or operations.

The Parties intend that, in relation to Club supporter personal data made available for the pilot or collected on the Club's behalf through the platform:

• the Club shall remain the data controller;
• Glasshouse shall act as data processor to the extent it processes such personal data on the Club's behalf;
• the Club shall retain ownership of such personal data; and
• the Parties shall enter into an appropriate data processing agreement before any live processing begins.

Nothing in this Letter of Intent transfers ownership of Club supporter personal data to Glasshouse.

Glasshouse shall retain ownership of:

• the 360Engage platform and all related intellectual property rights;
• the data models, taxonomies, scoring methodologies, segmentation logic, analytics structures, and other proprietary framework elements used by the platform; and
• the structured Fan Graph layer to the extent that it consists of Glasshouse's proprietary data architecture, derived relational framework, modelling structure, and aggregated or de-identified analytical schema, excluding any personal data owned or controlled by the Club.

Subject always to applicable law, the Club may grant Glasshouse limited rights to use Club supporter personal data as expressly set out in the definitive pilot agreement and data processing documentation. Those rights would be used only to deliver the pilot, produce agreed reporting and analytics, improve service performance for the Club, and exercise any other expressly agreed analytics rights.

Any rights of Glasshouse to use anonymised, aggregated, or de-identified outputs for product improvement, benchmarking, or internal analytics shall be subject to the definitive written agreement and must not allow the Club, its supporters, or any individual to be identified unless separately agreed in writing.

8. Intellectual Property

Except to the extent expressly agreed in a definitive written agreement, no licence, assignment, or transfer of intellectual property rights is granted by either Party under this Letter of Intent.

The Club retains all rights in its name, crest, marks, branding, content, materials, and data. Glasshouse retains all rights in the 360Engage platform, its software, workflows, documentation, methodologies, know-how, and related intellectual property, including any modifications, improvements, or derivative developments made during the Parties' discussions or any pilot.

9. Confidentiality

This Clause is intended to be legally binding.

Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by or on behalf of the other Party in connection with this Letter of Intent, the discovery process, pilot scoping discussions, and any proposed transaction or pilot.

A receiving Party shall not disclose the other Party's confidential information except to its employees, officers, professional advisers, prospective funders or investors, and actual or potential financing, strategic, or commercial counterparties who need the information to evaluate or support the proposed collaboration or Glasshouse's fundraising and business development activities, provided that each recipient is subject to confidentiality obligations or duties of confidence no less protective than those set out in this Letter of Intent.

The confidentiality obligations in this Clause do not apply to information which the receiving Party can demonstrate:

• is or becomes public other than through breach of this Letter of Intent;
• was lawfully known to it before disclosure;
• was lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction; or
• was independently developed without using the confidential information of the disclosing Party.

A Party may disclose confidential information where required by law, regulation, court order, or a competent authority, provided that, where legally permissible, it gives the other Party prompt notice before disclosure.

The confidentiality obligations in this Clause shall continue for two years from the date of this Letter of Intent.

Subject to the remainder of this Clause, the Club grants Glasshouse a limited right to refer to the Club by name in confidential investor, fundraising, and strategic partner discussions for the purpose of showing current commercial engagement and market interest in the 360Engage platform.

Glasshouse shall ensure that any such reference is fair, factual, and not misleading, and shall not state or imply that:

• the Club has endorsed the platform;
• the pilot has been agreed or launched; or
• any commercial outcome is assured,

unless the statement is accurate and separately agreed in writing.

Glasshouse shall not use the Club's crest, logos, trademarks, brand assets, publicity materials, quotations, or any public announcement naming the Club without the Club's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed for accurate and customary investor-reference wording.

If requested by the Club, Glasshouse shall provide the proposed wording for any investor-facing reference in advance and shall act reasonably in taking account of the Club's comments.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Letter of Intent and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, including any non-contractual dispute or claim, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Letter of Intent, including any non-contractual dispute or claim.

11. Costs and Counterparts

Each Party shall bear its own costs in connection with the preparation, negotiation, and contemplated implementation of this Letter of Intent and any related pilot documentation, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

This Letter of Intent may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which will be an original and all of which together will form one instrument. Signature by electronic means shall be effective.
