### Product Strategy & Vision

  - [**Core Problem:** Will explained that football clubs are "blind" to 60-70% of their supporter base. He shared a personal experience of spending £200 at his club's shop without the club capturing any of his data, nor connecting him with the club's charity he supports, highlighting a significant missed opportunity for engagement and revenue.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=1633.0)
  - [**Solution:** A platform that acts as a conduit for clubs to engage their own fans, not recreating a social media platform or asking clubs to give access to their fans, but rather giving clubs better access to fans they already have.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=1768.0)
  - [**Two Complementary Products:**](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2860.0)
    1.  **AI Voice Agent (Primary Focus):**
          - [**Inspiration:** Will's experience with an agentic AI company handling 5,000 simultaneous calls for screening interviews (e.g., BDO hiring in India).](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=1703.0)
          - [**Concept:** Train AI agents to represent a football club, offering 24/7 phone support and personalized conversations.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=1703.0)
          - [**Capabilities:** Gossip about the club, discuss upcoming signings, assist with travel planning (e.g., checking game schedule changes), suggest accommodation (e.g., club deals with Hilton Hotels), and facilitate pre-ordering merchandise (e.g., personalized jerseys to avoid match-day queues).](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2997.0)
          - [**Integration:** Designed to be embedded within the club's existing app or website, leveraging fan login data for personalized interactions (e.g., "Hey Will, how are you? Last time you called, you were coming over for the Arsenal game").](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2806.0)
          - [**Value:** Captures rich fan data for the club, enabling deeper engagement beyond match days.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2781.0)
    2.  **In-Stadium QR Companion:**
          - [**Purpose:** Enhance the game-day experience and revenue.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2285.0)
          - [**Capabilities:** In-seat food/merch ordering (e.g., pre-order burger and Coke for halftime), ticket purchases for future games, live betting (future consideration, acknowledging age-gating and licensing), and real-time promotions (e.g., "burgers are half-priced after the game in XYZ bar" via push notifications).](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2211.0)
          - [**Complementary:** While distinct from the voice agent, it serves to capture fans who might only interact during a game, feeding data into the broader system.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2860.0)
  - [**Club Partnership Model:**](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2298.0)
      - [**"Painless and Brainless":** Stewart emphasized that the solution must be easy for clubs, as most, especially smaller ones like Sutton, lack the manpower and backend administrative infrastructure to implement complex tech.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2298.0)
      - [**Our Role:** The company will handle all provisioning, onboarding, scripting the AI and conversations, and managing the backend.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2298.0)
      - [**Club's Role:** Clubs provide source data (e.g., accessibility maps, club narrative, specific rules for fan segregation) and are responsible for its accuracy. This ensures the system speaks in the club's language and adheres to their procedures.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=1989.0)
      - [**Benefits:** Allows clubs to learn more about their fans and "make more money off of them." It also improves fan experience by offering convenience and a sense of belonging.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=2478.0)
      - [**Monetization beyond sales:** Graham suggested connecting local businesses (e.g., vegan restaurants, Japanese food) for sponsorships, enabling clubs to get kickbacks for recommendations.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3146.0)

### Business Model & Urgency

  - [**Decision:** The business model is a SaaS (Software as a Service) subscription for clubs.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3205.0)
      - [**Rejected Alternative:** Shekhar proposed a free app with a transaction-fee model (e.g., 2-4% of sales).](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3205.0)
      - [**Rationale for SaaS:** Wil strongly dismissed the transaction-fee model as "shrapnel," arguing that it would misalign incentives and reduce the perceived value. He asserted, "we're selling a relationship between club and fan." The SaaS model positions the company as a strategic partner, and the long-term value lies in aggregating compelling fan data from 20-100+ clubs. This data will eventually enable the company to develop its own sports-specific Large Language Model (LLM) for projections and analytics, which clubs would subscribe to.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3205.0)
  - [**Urgency:** Graham conveyed extreme urgency, stating he has three potential investors and several clubs (including a close friend in football) waiting for a tangible product demo.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3377.0)
      - **Risk:** Without a working demo, he is "losing face" and risks being perceived as a "time waster" in the "incestuous industry" of football, jeopardizing potential investments and club partnerships. He noted an investor is calling him "on a daily basis" asking "where the \#@\! is it?"
      - [**Requirement:** An significantly enhanced demo is needed within one week. Graham clarified he wants to "leave them wanting more," not necessarily a fully finished product.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3489.0)
  - [**Meeting Frequency:** Graham stressed the need for regular communication, suggesting weekly meetings for Shekhar and at least 2-3 times a week for himself, Stewart, and Wil.](https://fathom.video/share/2tiCyN-zFwtzsupJeXpYXbGiVf8rMSPF?tab=summary&timestamp=3360.0)
